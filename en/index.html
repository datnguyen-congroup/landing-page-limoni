LIMONI — Italian for "lemons" — recalls the sun-drenched Mediterranean coast, where luxury lives in exquisite detail and heartfelt hospitality.

Set on An Thoi Avenue, the gateway to Sunset Town, Phu Quoc, LIMONI is the area's first true boutique hotel. Twenty individually styled rooms and an Italian restaurant-lounge made with local ingredients bring five-star service to guests who love beautiful things: romantic couples and young travellers with taste, looking for a stay that feels unique and private.

Restaurant ANFORA

Just as wine needs time to mature, deep love grows from experiences shared together — a dinner, a glass of wine, and honest words from the heart.

ANFORA, our Italian fusion restaurant on the ground floor, is a meeting place for every hour of the day. In the morning, start with a breakfast full of local flavour; at midday, linger over a slow-brewed coffee and feel the gentle rhythm of island life. As evening falls, it becomes an elegant dining destination for intimate dinners. The restaurant opens onto the rooftop, where you can watch the whole sunset in the late afternoon and Sunset Town's dazzling fireworks at night, in an unhurried, romantic corner of your own.
