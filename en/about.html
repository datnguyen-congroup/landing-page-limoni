The full essence of Italy,
under the Pearl Island sun

A perfect stay begins with the smile of the person who welcomes you, with the moment you sink back into a thoughtfully prepared room and the gentle scent of the tropics, through to a peaceful view of the sunset and unhurried dinners where heartfelt words are shared. LIMONI was born from a desire to offer personalised experiences, in a space designed with such care that it enchants even the most discerning guests.

Phu Quoc,
The pearl island of records

Phu Quoc is Vietnam's largest island, famous for its long white-sand beaches and some of Asia's most spectacular sunsets. In the 2026 Readers' Choice Awards, Condé Nast Traveler (US edition) named Phu Quoc the Best Island in Asia, with a score of 95.26/100.

The island is also home to the world's longest sea-crossing cable car — a Guinness record at nearly 8 km out to Hon Thom island — the lively Sunset Town, and the Kiss Bridge, an iconic bridge by Italian architect Marco Casamonti, inspired by Michelangelo's fresco “The Creation of Adam”. In 2027, Phu Quoc will host the APEC Summit, putting the pearl island on the world map. LIMONI sits right at the heart of it all, in An Thoi, the gateway to Sunset Town on the island's southern tip.

A land of love,
art and emotion

A nostalgic spirit and a cinematic mood create a slow, deeply felt rhythm of stay. LIMONI is shaped as a hideaway with a strong personal signature, where emotion rises from the interplay of light, materials and depth of space rather than outward display.

Deep-toned walls set off classical works of art; warm golden light filters through sheer voile curtains; commanding burgundy, deep sapphire and the gleam of brass become the emotional palette throughout. Where nostalgic arched architecture, eclectic materials and refined living meet, the space is at once mysterious, seductive and stylish — inviting you to step fully into the luxurious world of old Europe.

Eclectic Maximalism

Inspired by a nostalgic beauty, where light, fine materials and a deep palette create an artful space, LIMONI is shaped in the Eclectic Maximalist style — a graceful meeting of classical elegance and the free, characterful spirit of a contemporary boutique hotel.

Warm, deep tones such as burgundy, sapphire and wood brown, touched with the charm of brass, feel cosy and relaxing yet utterly captivating. The eclectic spirit lets the space break away from familiar templates, while the richness of detail (maximalism) never takes away its calm, gently leading you into a peaceful, natural hideaway full of memories.

The Love Post Office

The Nostalgic Post Office

Inspired by the old post offices of the last century, where time seemed to stand still for handwritten letters and love's whispers, LIMONI's lobby steps away from the usual reception desk. Dark wooden cabinets with finely divided pigeonholes, a deep-red postbox, vintage luggage trunks and a classic clock face recall an era of journeys and romantic waiting.

More than a stopover, this is a place that invites you to slow down and reach deeper feelings. In the quiet, couples write their own love letters, fold in their thoughts, seal them with a wax stamp bearing their own emblem, and drop them into the hotel's memory postbox. Under soft golden light, exchanging letters becomes a ritual that connects two souls, turning the stay into a romantic film in which every guest writes the script of their own memories.
