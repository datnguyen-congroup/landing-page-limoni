ANFORA
(by LIMONI)

An Italian fusion restaurant on the ground floor and rooftop, where Phu Quoc's natural ingredients meet the generous spirit of Italian and Mediterranean cooking, in an elegant, open space — a one-of-a-kind dining destination in the south of the pearl island. A place for food lovers and romantic souls.

Anfora The ancient amphora

The amphora was the two-handled clay vessel of the ancient Greeks and Romans, used to hold wine and olive oil. It opens the visual story of the restaurant: an open kitchen connecting the cooking space with the dining room, a sleek bar on a fine stone counter, and facing it a carefully curated wine cabinet — a display that is easy to remember and to talk about.

Carrying on the spirit of LIMONI — the Mediterranean's "lemons" — ANFORA is the culinary chapter of the brand.

The taste of Emotion

The open kitchen and bar sit right in the dining room, so you can watch every dish being cooked and every drink being mixed. Whether it is an energising breakfast, a slow-brewed coffee or an intimate dinner, comfortable seating and a relaxed atmosphere always invite you to stay a little longer, talk without hurry, and let down your guard to share your thoughts and feelings with the people you love.

Culinary philosophy

Steak and local seafood, Italian pizza and pasta, refreshing desserts. Vegetarian dishes throughout the menu.

1.

Mediterranean fusion cuisine

We draw inspiration from the free spirit of Mediterranean cooking, where fresh ingredients and natural flavours always take centre stage. Pairing that finesse with local produce and creative technique, every dish becomes a meeting of cultures — new yet harmonious, simple yet memorable.

2.

Fresh Phu Quoc seafood

For us, the taste of the island begins with Phu Quoc's fresh seafood. Respecting the natural sweetness and character of each ingredient, we cook just enough to let the freshness shine rather than masking it with seasoning. Every dish is a simple story of the sea, bringing the spirit and abundance of the pearl island to your table.

A private space

The VIP Rooftop is a private space on the roof terrace for intimate gatherings of 10–20 guests. Dining is by reservation only, with a set menu chosen from ANFORA's menu and special dishes, prepared by the restaurant's own kitchen team.

You are also welcome to come up to the rooftop for a coffee, to relax and chat at leisure in the open air.
