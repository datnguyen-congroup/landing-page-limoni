The Love Archive

Telling love stories
through the language of design

Each floor has five rooms, numbered 01 to 05. Those five positions correspond to five distinct designs; the layout repeats from floor to floor, but different styling gives every room its own character.

Twenty rooms are the twenty chapters of The Love Archive, from The Letter to The Empty Frame. Each room tells its own story through painting, light and the feelings of those who experience it.

The LoveArchive

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

Check-in is from 14:00. Early check-in may be possible depending on availability — please contact us in advance and we will do our best to help. On arrival, our reception team will welcome you and show you to your room.

Check-out time and accepted payment methods are stated in your booking confirmation email. If you need a late check-out, please let reception know in advance and we will arrange it subject to availability.

ANFORA, our ground-floor restaurant, serves everything from morning coffee to dinner with wine, and welcomes both hotel guests and visitors. Vegetarian dishes are available throughout the menu.

From anniversary room decorations to a private rooftop dinner for groups of 10–20 (by reservation only), you can note your request in the booking form or contact LIMONI directly.

LIMONI is at 62-64 An Thoi Avenue, Phu Quoc Special Zone, An Giang Province, Vietnam. Please contact us in advance for transfers and parking.

Our cancellation policy, policies for staying with children and other house rules are sent with your booking confirmation email. For any questions, please call +84 348 779 988 or email info@limoniboutiquehotel.com.
